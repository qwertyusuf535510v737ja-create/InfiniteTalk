## 2. Kanal tavsifi (About)

Nusxa oling, o'zgartirmang:

```
Bare Facts explains what actually happens to the human body — and to living
systems — when conditions go past the limit. No dramatisation, no guesswork.
Every episode is built from published research, and every source is listed
in the video description.

Three kinds of episodes:
· The Limit — what the body does at extreme altitude, depth, cold and heat.
· The Mechanism — how a disease, a toxin, or an exposure actually kills.
· The Survivors — who lived, why they lived, and what the statistics say.

New episode every 8 days.

This channel uses a synthetic presenter. The voice and the on-screen host are
AI-generated. The research, the script and the source selection are done by a
human. This is disclosed on every video.

Business: [email]
```
